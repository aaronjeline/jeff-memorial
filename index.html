About This Map

In honor of a life filled with adventure, curiosity, and a love of travel, we invite you to take a sticker with you on your journey. When you place it somewhere meaningful, you become part of a shared memorial — a collection of places, memories, and moments that help his spirit live on around the world.

Use this page to record where you placed your sticker. Each entry becomes part of a growing map of remembrance, connection, and love.

Each sticker represents a small act of remembrance and a shared connection. Together, these travels and tributes create a living memorial — one that reflects the places explored, the memories cherished, and the lasting impact of a life well lived.

How to Use

Click anywhere on the map to place a pin. Enter your name, and the memory will be saved with the date and location.

You can also tap the crosshair button in the top-right corner of the map to drop a pin at your current location.
